# DATA DICTIONARY 
*Describes the columns and information in the data [file](<Hotdog Survey (Responses) - Form Responses 1.csv>)*


| Column| Description| Potential Reponses|                   
|-------|------------|-------------------|
| Timestamp | This holds the timestamp data of when the individual filled out the form. |month/day/year, hour:minute:second|
| Year at UVA | The year that the student is at UVA | (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th) or Graduate/beyond. |
| Gender Identity | Contains gender information about the individual|  e.g., man, woman, nonbinary. |
| UVA School| The speciality school of UVA that the individual is in  | e.g., Engineering, McIntire, Arts & Sciences, etc. |
| Is a hot dog a sandwich? | Yes or no answer or whether a hot dog is a sandwich. | Yes/No|
| Strength of feelings | How strong they feel about their opinion. | Scale of 1-5 |
| Frequency of  hot dog consumption| The frequency of hot dog consumption by the individual | e.g. never, occassionally, rarely, etc. |
