# DATA DICTIONARY 
*Describes the columns and information in the data [file](<Hotdog Survey (Responses) - Form Responses 1.csv>)*

### Timestamp
This holds the timestamp data of when the individual filled out the form.

### Year at UVA
The year that the student is at UVA (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th) or Graduate/beyond.

### Gender Identity
Contains gender information about the individual e.g., main, woman, nonbinary.

### UVA School
The speciality school of UVA that the individual is in e.g., Engineering, McIntire, Arts & Sciences, etc.

### Is a hot dog a sandwich?
Yes or no answer or whether a hot dog is a sandwich.

### Strength of feelings
A scale of 1-5 about how strong they feel about their opinion.

### Frequency of  hot dog consumption
The frequency of hot dog consumption by the individual e.g. never, occassionally, rarely, etc.
